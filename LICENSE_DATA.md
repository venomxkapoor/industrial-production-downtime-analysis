## Important Data Notes

- The workbook `production_raw.xlsx` contains preprocessed operational summaries (`daily_operation_summary`, `processed_hourly`, `hourly_operation_breakdown`) and a stop-event log (`downtime_event_log`), rather than raw PLC sensor streams.
- All records in `data/production_raw.xlsx` are preserved verbatim without alteration or synthetic additions.

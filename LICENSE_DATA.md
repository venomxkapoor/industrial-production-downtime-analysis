# Dataset Licence and Provenance

## Official Source

- **Dataset Title**: Industrial Production Time-Series Dataset from a Beverage Bottling Line
- **Observation Period**: 2022–2023 (59 operating runs across 56 unique dates from 2022-07-22 to 2023-02-10)
- **Repository**: Zenodo
- **DOI**: `10.5281/zenodo.18146866`
- **URL**: https://zenodo.org/records/18146866
- **Source File**: `data/production_raw.xlsx`

## Important Data Notes

- The workbook `production_raw.xlsx` contains preprocessed operational summaries (`daily_operation_summary`, `processed_hourly`, `hourly_operation_breakdown`) and a stop-event log (`downtime_event_log`), rather than raw PLC sensor streams.
- All records in `data/production_raw.xlsx` are preserved verbatim without alteration or synthetic additions.
